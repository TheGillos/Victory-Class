# VICTORY CLASS — DECK-BY-DECK LAYOUT

USS Victory, NCC-9754-A · Heavy Interdictor · 455m × 280m × ~49m · 14 decks
Draft 2 — reconciled against hull geometry and the Project Dossier

---

## GOVERNING CONSTRAINTS

Fourteen decks in a lifting-body wedge this flat yields roughly 3.5m of deck
pitch, and the hull tapers hard toward the nose and the outboard edges. Decks 1
and 14 are therefore equipment spaces, not decks — 1.9m crawl height, no
habitation, EVA access only.

Over 65% of internal volume is uninhabitable. The habitable envelope is a
compact block: Decks 2–6 forward and amidships, plus the Main Engineering volume
aft on Decks 5–7. Everything else is machinery a crewman visits in a suit.

**Tandem cores are mounted HORIZONTALLY.** The hull is too flat for a vertical
core stack. Core A (port) and Core B (starboard) run fore-and-aft along the
walls of Main Engineering, separated by the subspace displacement manifold.
Injector heads forward, ejection doors aft through the armored transom.

**The AID block is aftmost.** Bulkhead 4-ALPHA separates Main Engineering from
the Advanced Impulse Drive and its EPS capacitor banks. Engineering is forward
of that line; AID and capacitors are behind it. Nobody stands near 4-ALPHA
during an afterburner burn.

## DECK 1 — DORSAL EQUIPMENT SPACE
*Uninhabitable. 1.9m. Pressurized, unheated.*

- Retractable armored sensor dome — retracts flush and seals for Cold Run
- Upper metaphasic shield grid emitters
- Dorsal thermal radiator vane roots, explosive decoupling bolts
- SIF node cluster A (armor matrix)

> Deck 1 exceeds 340K during sustained Cold Run. Suited access only.

## DECK 2 — COMMAND
- **Armored CIC** — sunken bridge module, "Cicada" tritanium blast shutters
  (1.2s seal on Red Alert; navigation thereafter is sensor telemetry only)
- Captain's ready room and quarters
- Tactical planning room / holo-tank
- Dorsal docking port and personnel airlock — primary crew ingress

> No viewports anywhere on this class. The CIC aperture is the only transparency
> in the hull, and it spends most of combat behind 40cm of tritanium.

## DECK 3 — SENIOR OFFICERS / UPPER AFT MACHINERY
- *Fwd:* Senior officer quarters, officer's mess, XO's office
- *Mid:* Turbolift junction, upper computer core service access
- *Aft:* AID plasma venting control gallery; radiator coolant pump gallery
- *Aft-most:* "Flash-Evac" probe silos — upper maintenance level

## DECK 4 — MEDICAL / ARMORY / ENGINEERING OVERLOOK
- *Fwd:* Sickbay — 12 biobeds, two wards, surgical suite, EMH Mk IV
  processing core, and a morgue (four drawers; this is a warship)
- *Fwd:* Emergency Transporter Room 5 — casualty beam-in, direct sickbay access
- *Mid:* Main armory; CQC holo-simulator; EVA suit lockers
- *Aft:* Main Engineering overlook — matter injector heads, Cores A and B
- *Aft of 4-ALPHA:* AID EPS capacitor bank, upper tier

## DECK 5 — HABITATION / ENGINEERING CONTROL
- *Fwd:* Enlisted quarters — private rooms, oversized by fleet standard
- *Fwd:* Crew lounge, informally **"The Constellation Room"** (the plaque from
  the original NCC-9754 hangs behind the bar)
- *Mid:* Holodecks 1 and 2, hardened emitters
- *Aft:* **Main Engineering — upper level.** Primary control room, Chief
  Engineer's office, overlooking the core volume below
- *Aft of 4-ALPHA:* AID magnetic constriction coil assembly

## DECK 6 — CREW SUPPORT / CORE CENTERLINE
- *Fwd:* Enlisted quarters, main mess and galley, recycling
- *Mid:* Transporter Rooms 1 and 2 (six-pad, rapid-cycle); Cargo Transporter 1
- *Mid:* **Secondary Command Station** — armored, self-contained, can fly and
  fight the ship if the CIC is lost
- *Aft:* Main Engineering mid-level — core centerlines, plasma transfer
  conduits, subspace displacement manifold access
- *Aft-most:* Flash-Evac probe launch mechanisms and mesh transceiver arrays

## DECK 7 — ENGINEERING FLOOR / COMPUTER / CLOAK
- *Fwd:* Upper torpedo magazine, rotary feed A
- *Mid:* Main computer core, upper level — quad-redundant isolinear/bio-neural,
  gel pack galleries
- *Center:* **Mark-II "Alliance" cloak generator room.** Thermally isolated
  behind its own airlock; crew call it "the Icebox." Access by CO authorization
- *Aft:* Main Engineering floor — antimatter injection relays, core ejection
  track, aft blowout doors

## DECK 8 — SECURITY / PRIMARY FORWARD WEAPONS
- *Fwd:* 4× Class-X pulse phaser cannons — mounts, pre-fire chambers, direct
  EPS taps, recessed into the nose notch
- *Mid:* Main computer core, lower level; ESH sub-processors
- *Mid:* Main security center, brig (four cells), ESH muster points
- *Aft:* Engineering subfloor conduit gallery

## DECK 9 — HEAVY ORDNANCE
- *Fwd:* 2× rapid-fire quantum torpedo launchers, rotary autoloaders
- *Fwd/Mid:* Main magazine — 250 quantum, 100 photon
- *Mid:* **Ordnance loading trunk.** External armored hatch mating to a
  starbase or tender ordnance arm. Six-hour evolution; ship must be cold and
  the magazine inerted. There is no way to rearm this ship underway
- *Mid:* Cargo Bay 1 — ground-assault munitions, secure stowage
- *Aft:* Aft launcher and aft magazine

## DECK 10 — DEFLECTOR / INTERDICTION / STORES
- *Fwd:* Main navigational deflector, upper level
- *Fwd:* **Subspace Pulse Emitter** — primary array, deflector power shunts
- *Mid:* Cargo Bay 2 — 90-day consumables, general stores
- *Mid:* Cargo Transporter 2; ventral cargo/personnel airlock and docking port
- *Aft:* Deuterium bunkerage, upper

## DECK 11 — AUXILIARY POWER / FUEL
- *Fwd:* Main navigational deflector, lower level
- *Mid:* 4× auxiliary fusion reactors, dedicated to shield and weapon redundancy
- *Aft:* Primary deuterium tankage; antimatter storage pods, upper tier

## DECK 12 — VENTRAL SENSORS / TRANSPORT / ANTIMATTER
- *Fwd:* Ventral sensor array and targeting scanners
- *Mid:* Transporter Rooms 3 and 4
- *Mid/Aft:* Antimatter storage pods, lower tier; emergency ejection rails
- Ventral metaphasic shield generators

> Deck 12 is the lowest deck served by turbolift.

## DECK 13 — THERMAL & STRUCTURAL MANAGEMENT
*Uninhabitable during Cold Run.*

- Liquid helium reservoirs and heavy-duty coolant pumps feeding the dorsal and
  nacelle flush-deck radiator vanes
- **Chemical heat-sink banks** — the sinks that absorb waste heat under cloak.
  Their thermal capacity *is* the cloak's duration limit; everything else about
  the Mark-II is unlimited, and this deck is why it isn't
- Primary SIF generators, tuned for the 18cm ablative matrix
- Main atmospheric processing and life support

> Deck 13 passes 350K within forty minutes of a hot Cold Run. Suited access,
> buddy system, hard time limits.

## DECK 14 — VENTRAL EQUIPMENT SPACE
*Uninhabitable. 1.9m.*

- Lower ablative armor matrix; replaceable plate tracks
- Tractor beam emitter
- Anti-proton sweep emitters (counter-cloak detection)
- Workbee / maintenance drone bay — 4 units, unpressurized, ventral hatch.
  This is the closest thing the Victory has to a hangar
- Ventral EVA airlock

## VERTICAL CIRCULATION

Three turbolift shafts, and they matter more than usual on this ship:

- **Port Main** and **Starboard Main** — Decks 2 through 12
- **The Spine** — an armored express shaft connecting the CIC (Deck 2) directly
  to Main Engineering (Deck 7) and nothing in between, sealed at both ends
  behind blast doors. Command and engineering can reach each other when the
  rest of the ship is depressurized

Below Deck 12 there is no turbolift at all. Decks 13 and 14 are reached by
Jefferies tube and ladderway, in EVA gear.

**On the tubes:** 18cm of armor plus coolant runs and EPS trunking squeeze the
Jefferies network down to 0.8m crown-to-deck, against the 1.1m fleet standard.
Damage control parties are selected partly on build. It is a running complaint
in every engineering department that has served aboard.

## ACCESS & EGRESS — COMPLETE LIST

| Point | Deck | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Dorsal docking port + airlock | 2 | Primary crew ingress/egress |
| Ventral docking port + cargo airlock | 10 | Cargo, personnel, tender ops |
| Ordnance loading trunk | 9 | Torpedo replenishment, cold only |
| Workbee hatch | 14 | Maintenance drones, unpressurized |
| Ventral EVA airlock | 14 | Hull work |
| Transporters (5 rooms, 2 cargo) | 4, 6, 12 | Normal operations |

**No shuttlecraft. No captain's yacht. No escape pods.**

Egress in an abandon-ship is Flash-Evac or nothing. Starfleet Medical filed a
formal objection to the certification on exactly this point and was overruled by
the Advanced Tactical Design Bureau in the same week the ship was commissioned.

## HABITABLE ENVELOPE — SUMMARY

Crew live, work, and eat in Decks 2–6 forward and amidships, and in the Main
Engineering volume on Decks 5–7 aft. That is the entire ship as far as daily
life is concerned: roughly a third of a hull that is a third of a kilometer
long.

Everything forward of the magazine, everything below Deck 12, and everything aft
of Bulkhead 4-ALPHA is machinery — visited on a work order, in a suit, with a
time limit.
